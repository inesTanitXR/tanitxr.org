## 💰 GRANTS & FUNDING

### 🖼 Art Worker Artist Grant 2026 — ARTIST ENGINEERING 🚨

📅 Deadline: September 14, 2026 — or as soon as the first 500 applications arrive, so don't wait
📍 Global / Online
👥 Artists whose livelihood is connected to art-industry work — museums, galleries, artist studios, fabrication, framing, conservation, art logistics, storage, and more
✏️ A biennial $5,000 AUD grant supporting the artistic practice of the people who keep the art world running. The application is intentionally minimal — a brief statement and a link to your work. No required outcomes and no grant acquittal.
👉 Apply: https://artistengineering.co/pages/awa

### 💸 Fast Forward Accelerator 2027 🚨

📅 Deadline: September 18, 2026 (11:59 pm PT) — extended
📍 Remote program (Feb–June 2027) with in-person kickoff and Demo Day in San Francisco
👥 Early-stage tech or AI-powered nonprofits with a working MVP — anywhere in the world, fiscally sponsored teams included
✏️ The only accelerator built just for tech nonprofits: a $25K+ unrestricted grant, three months of curriculum on both the tech and the nonprofit side, mentors, and a Demo Day in front of funders. Their own words: "women and people of color often hold back if they're not a perfect match on paper — send in that app."
👉 Apply: https://www.ffwd.org/accelerator

### 🌍 Awesome Foundation — Conservation & Climate Micro-Grants

📅 Next deadlines: September 25 and November 27, 2026
📍 Global / Online
👥 Anyone — individuals, collectives, nonprofits — no registration or paperwork needed
✏️ $1,000, no strings attached, for projects about climate, habitat, ecotourism or environmental communication. Six rounds a year. If your art or XR project tells a climate story, this is the easiest $1,000 you'll ever apply for.
👉 Apply: https://www.awesomefoundation.org/en/chapters/conservationandclimate

### 🧬 Ars Biologica ArtXScience Residency 2027–2028

📅 Deadline: September 28, 2026 (midnight CET)
📍 Hybrid — three stays in České Budějovice, Czechia (European Capital of Culture 2028) + remote development
👥 Artists, designers and collectives of any nationality, 18+, with 5+ years of practice and some art–science or community experience; must be able to travel in the EU
✏️ Two positions worth €24,000 each: €6,000 artist fee, up to €15,000 production, up to €3,000 travel plus accommodation, from January 2027 to April 2028. Run by Ars Electronica — and they explicitly want spatial, installation and immersive work, not screen-based pieces.
👉 Apply: https://ars.electronica.art/export/en/ecoc2028-ars-biologica-art-science-residency/

## 🎨 CALLS FOR IMMERSIVE ART & FILM

### 🕶 SXSW 2027 — XR Experience

📅 Deadlines: September 15 ($70) · final October 1, 2026 ($90)
📍 Austin, Texas — March 15–21, 2027
👥 VR, MR and AR creators, immersive performance, projection and audio installations, AI installations — "anywhere technology and art converge"
✏️ One of the most visible XR showcases in North America. You'll need a streamable 360 or 2D playthrough link; selections are announced by February 9, 2027.
👉 Submit: https://sxsw.com/film-submissions/

### 🎬 Sundance Film Festival 2027 — Feature Films

📅 Late deadline: September 21, 2026 ($125)
📍 Boulder, Colorado — January 21–31, 2027 (Sundance's first year in its new home)
👥 Feature films 50+ minutes with world or international premiere status; documentary and experimental features are also considered for the New Frontier section
✏️ Shorts and episodic have closed, but features are still open until the 21st. If you've been sitting on a finished documentary or an experimental feature, this is the window. (Note: there's no separate XR category anymore — submit as a feature.)
👉 Submit: https://www.sundance.org/festivals/sundance-film-festival/submit/

### 👓 FIVARS 2027 — Festival of International VR & AR Stories

📅 Earlybird: September 24 · regular November 24 · late December 24, 2026 · extended January 24, 2027
📍 Los Angeles + online — February 22 – March 9, 2027
👥 VR and MR stories, 360° cinema, live and spatial performance, WebXR and installation work
✏️ One of the most artist-friendly XR festivals around — they've shown work from 23 countries and they accept WebXR, so you don't need a headset distribution deal to be selected. Notifications February 7.
👉 Submit: https://fivars.net/

## 🚀 PROGRAMS & PITCHES

### 🚀 UnitedXR Europe 2026 — Booster Programs 🚨

📅 Deadline: September 15, 2026 — acceptance confirmed October 15
📍 Hybrid — online masterclasses in mid-November, then on-site in Brussels, December 7–9, 2026
👥 XR creators with early-stage narrative, artistic or documentary projects (Narratives), indie AR/VR game studios (Games), developers with an MVP (Builders), early-stage XR+AI startups (Startups)
✏️ Four acceleration tracks with expert mentorship, a live pitch on the UnitedXR stage, 3 guaranteed 15-minute meetings with decision-makers, nightly networking and a full event pass. Up to 3 team members per project. Free to apply; a participation fee (€300/€200/€100 per team member) applies only if you're selected. Startups can also enter the free Startup Pitch Competition — same deadline.
👉 Apply: https://unitedxr.eu/booster-programs

## 🏆 AWARDS

### 🏆 World Summit Awards 2026 — Culture & Heritage, Learning, Inclusion

📅 Deadline: September 30, 2026 (national round)
📍 Online nomination — winners attend the WSA Global Congress in spring 2027
👥 Anyone with a launched digital solution (2022 or later) that has measurable social impact — from any UN member state; under-26 founders can enter the Young Innovators track
✏️ The UN-affiliated awards for digital impact, with eight categories including Culture & Heritage, Learning & Education, and Inclusion & Empowerment. Free to enter. The 40 global winners get the Congress, mentoring, pitching and international press. If you built something for your community, nominate it.
👉 Enter: https://wsa-global.org/contest-global-award/

## 🎤 SPEAK, PRESENT, BUILD

### 🎤 IEEE VR 2027 — Call for Workshop Proposals

📅 Deadline: September 28, 2026 (notification October 12)
📍 Melbourne, Australia — workshops February 27–28, 2027
👥 Researchers and practitioners — a 3-page proposal is all it takes
✏️ You don't need to be a professor to organize a workshop at the biggest academic VR conference. Propose a half-day session — XR for cultural heritage, XR in the Global South, community-driven documentation — and you get a program slot, a public call under your name, and optional publication of the workshop papers in IEEE Xplore. No submission fee.
👉 Details: https://ieeevr.org/2027/contribute/workshops/

### 💻 Immerse the Bay 2026 — Stanford XR Hackathon

📅 Priority deadline: October 2 · final October 16, 2026
📍 Stanford University, California — November 13–15, 2026
👥 All skill levels and backgrounds; you don't need to be a Stanford student
✏️ 36 hours, around 400 hackers, $300k+ of loaned headsets and hardware, 10+ industry workshops and a prize pool that topped $30k last year. The application takes less than 10 minutes.
👉 Apply: https://portal.immersethebay.org/

## 🇹🇳 FOR TUNISIANS & NORTH AFRICANS

### 🎓 Chevening Scholarships 2027/28 — Tunisia

📅 Deadline: October 6, 2026 (11:00 UTC)
📍 United Kingdom — one-year master's
👥 Tunisian citizens with a degree, at least two years of work experience, and a plan to return home; no upper age limit
✏️ Fully funded: tuition, living stipend, flights and visa. UK master's programmes in digital heritage, HCI, game design and immersive media are all eligible. This is the scholarship I wish someone had told me about earlier.
👉 Apply: https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/tunisia/

### 🎨 Culture Resource (Al Mawred) — Production Awards 2027

📅 Deadline: October 19, 2026 (16:00 Beirut time)
📍 Arab region — open to the diaspora too
👥 Artists and writers under 35 who originate from an Arab country, living in the region or abroad
✏️ Twenty-five production grants a year: up to €8,500 for visual arts, music and sound art, performing arts and literature, up to €13,000 for cinema. XR and new-media projects fit under visual arts or cinema. The work has to take place in or benefit the Arab region. Results end of March 2027.
👉 Apply: https://mawred.org/artistic-creativity/production-awards/?lang=en
