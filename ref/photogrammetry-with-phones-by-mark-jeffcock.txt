 Splats With Phones 
[p] This 6-week online course introduces splats using smartphones, taught by Mark Jeffcock. 
[p] Participants will learn how to capture real-world objects using a phone, turn them into 3D models and Gaussian splats, and review scans together in an immersive learning environment. 
[p] The cohort meets once a week at 7PM Tunisia Time (2PM Eastern) for 1 hour, starting August 26th, for 6 weeks. No prior experience is required. Attendance and engagement matter more than technical background. 
[p] Due to limited spots, we will review applications holistically based on availability, background, and motivation. More details will be shared with accepted participants.
