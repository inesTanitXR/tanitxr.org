## 💰 GRANTS, PRIZES & PAID WORK

### 🦊 Mozilla Foundation Fellowships 2027

📅 Deadline: Letter of Intent by December 17, 2026, 11:59 pm Pacific (full application by invitation)

📍 Remote, 12 months from June 1, 2027

👥 Anyone, any country. Free. One spot is reserved for an artist-provocateur (Creative Advocacy), and work outside the focus areas can apply as a Wildcard

✏️ Ten fellowships for people building alternatives in technology. Independent track: USD 125,000 (USD 100,000 stipend plus USD 25,000 project budget). Embedded track with a host organization: USD 100,000.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/mozilla-foundation-fellowships-2027/?ref=nl22

───────────────────────────────────────

## 🏆 AWARDS

### 🎥 Sony Future Filmmaker Awards 2027, Immersive Category

📅 Deadline: December 15, 2026, 7:00 am CST

📍 Online entry; shortlisted filmmakers go to a four-day workshop in the US, flights and accommodation covered

👥 Any nationality; under-18s need consent. 3D, 180° or 360° video, 5 to 20 minutes, released January 1, 2025 to December 15, 2026. AR, MR and XR pieces are not eligible. Free

✏️ New Immersive category presented by Meta, up to three films shortlisted; the prize amount is not stated on the site. Fiction, Non-Fiction, Animation, Student and Future Format categories run alongside.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/sony-future-filmmaker-awards-2027-immersive-category/?ref=nl22

───────────────────────────────────────

## 🎤 SHOW YOUR WORK: FESTIVALS & EXHIBITIONS

### 🌴 FilmGate Interactive 13, Miami

📅 Deadline: regular December 20, 2026 (USD 35) · late January 18, 2027

📍 Miami and Miami Beach, March 19 to 24, 2027

👥 Open worldwide, any length, fiction or non-fiction

✏️ Categories include VR, AR, immersive experience, interactive documentary, interactive installation, dome experience, public immersive installation, and new games and applications. Entries need a budget estimate, technical rider and spatial requirements. Notification February 1, 2027.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/filmgate-interactive-13-miami-vr-ar-dome-and-interactive-wor/?ref=nl22

### 🔮 Fulldome Festival Montevideo 2027

📅 Deadline: December 19, 2026 (results January 10, 2027)

📍 Montevideo, Uruguay, April 8 to 11, 2027; the work travels, you don't have to

👥 Artists and collectives worldwide, no age limit. Free with the code FDFM2027 on FilmFreeway

✏️ Fulldome works only (1:1 domemaster), up to 12 minutes, finished after 2021; AI-made content must be declared.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/fulldome-festival-montevideo-2027/?ref=nl22

## 📄 PAPERS & CONFERENCES

### 🎓 UNESCO Digital Learning Week 2027: Call for Proposals

📅 Deadline: December 9, 2026

📍 UNESCO Headquarters, Paris, March 23 to 25, 2027. In person only; no fees, but you cover your own travel and lodging

👥 Educators, researchers, organisations and makers of learning tools already in use. Early pilots and purely conceptual ideas are not accepted

✏️ Panels, strategy labs, trainings and futures workshops, 24 live demo slots and 15 "Classroom" interactive teaching sessions, plus a "Surprise us" track. Decisions January 11 to February 1, 2027.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/unesco-digital-learning-week-2027-call-for-proposals/?ref=nl22

### 🥽 IEEE VR 2027, 3DUI Contest: Beyond Shared Reality

📅 Deadline: register by December 11, 2026; abstract and video by December 16, 2026 (AoE)

📍 Submit online; finalists present on site at IEEE VR 2027

👥 Anyone, teams of up to ten. No contest fee, but one member must register for the conference

✏️ A contest for new 3D interface techniques for shared spatial interaction across different immersive setups.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/ieee-vr-2027-3dui-contest-beyond-shared-reality/?ref=nl22

## 🏠 RESIDENCIES

### 🏺 IN-SITU Artist Residencies in the Medina of Tunis and Zaghouan

📅 Deadline: December 20, 2026 (proposals reviewed every two months, so earlier is better)

📍 Medina of Tunis and Zaghouan, Tunisia, stays of up to 10 days until March 2027

👥 Individual artists of any nationality with provable experience; new media is eligible. Free

✏️ Four to six residencies for research and new work tied to local heritage and communities, part of the EU-funded IN-SITU project. Average budget about EUR 6,000 each, artist fee and production included.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/in-situ-artist-residencies-in-the-medina-of-tunis-and-zaghou/?ref=nl22
