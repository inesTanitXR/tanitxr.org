## ⏳ CLOSING THIS WEEKEND

### 🏛 AIA Site Preservation Grant 2027

📅 Deadline: November 1, 2026

📍 Any archaeological site; remote application

👥 Projects that preserve a site and involve the local community; no nationality limit stated. Free

✏️ Up to $15,000 from the Archaeological Institute of America for conservation planning (including photographic and digital documentation), hands-on conservation, preventive measures and monitoring, training, and public outreach. Stronger applications show community involvement and a long-term maintenance plan. Awards announced by March 1, 2027.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/aia-site-preservation-grant-2027/?ref=nl21

## 💰 GRANTS

### 📝 National Geographic Society, The Human Thread RFP

📅 Deadline: December 1, 2026

📍 Remote, project anywhere

👥 Applicants 18 or over who lead the project themselves, any country except those under sanctions. Projects start at least six months after the deadline. Free

✏️ Request for proposals for storytelling projects on the origins and present-day forms of human cultures, funded with support from Lilly Endowment. Funding tiers are shown inside the National Geographic portal once you start an application.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/national-geographic-society-the-human-thread-rfp/?ref=nl21

## 🎨 RESIDENCIES

### 🎨 Bogliasco Foundation Fellowship, Fall 2027

📅 Deadline: December 1, 2026 (Spring 2028 round: March 11, 2027)

📍 In person, Bogliasco Study Center on the Ligurian coast near Genoa, Italy, one semester

👥 Artists and scholars in the arts and humanities, any nationality; conversational English needed. USD 30 until a week before the deadline, then USD 45

✏️ Full room and board and a work space. Travel, materials and personal expenses are yours.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/bogliasco-foundation-fellowship-fall-2027/?ref=nl21

## 🇹🇳 FOR TUNISIANS & NORTH AFRICANS

### 🇹🇳 Changemakers for Youth 2027 (ChangemakerXchange)

📅 Deadline: November 9, 2026 (no time given)

📍 Online sessions, then a five-day summit in the region in mid-March 2027 (lodging, meals and local transport covered, travel stipend depending on funding)

👥 Ages 18 to 35 who live and work in Tunisia, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Morocco, Palestine, Switzerland or Germany (German applicants need a project with impact in Eastern Germany), and hold a leadership role in a youth-focused social or environmental venture. Free

✏️ Three-year programme for 80 changemakers: capacity building, the summit, seed funding for cross-border projects and membership of the ChangemakerXchange network. Interviews November 20 to December 19; decisions January 13, 2027.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/changemakers-for-youth-2027-changemakerxchange/?ref=nl21
