# Cancelling the old site's subscriptions

Written 2026-10-01. Findings are in `old-site-subscriptions.md`; this is the order of work.

## Before cancelling anything: turn off auto-renew, do not delete

Almost every vendor here offers two different buttons, and they are not the same thing.

- **Turn off auto-renew** keeps the licence until the term you already paid for runs out, and
  stops the next charge. This is almost always the one you want.
- **Cancel / delete the licence** ends it immediately and usually forfeits the rest of the term.

Unless you are inside a refund window, choose auto-renew off.

## And check the renewal date first

If something renewed in the last 14 to 30 days you may still be inside the vendor's refund
window, and a refund is worth far more than switching off a renewal you have already paid.
Elementor and Crocoblock both publish refund policies. The receipts are in the Titan
mailboxes, `ines@tanitxr.org` and `info@tanitxr.org`, not in Gmail.

So: read the receipt, then decide, then click.
