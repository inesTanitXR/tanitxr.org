## 6. Writing rules for anything about Ines or Tanit XR

- Warm, simple, excited and grateful, first person. Always credit the volunteers and team.
- No em dashes. No clever taglines, no "not X but Y" lines, no aphorisms.
- Ines is Tunisian, not Tunisian-American. She is from Nabeul; do not call the Neapolis ruins "Roman."
- Never center archive numbers in outreach or bios. Draw on the fuller picture: artist, speaker, community, research (inessaid.com, tanitxr.org).
- Never describe Tanit XR's work as "full documentation of a site."
- El Jem amphitheater is about 1,800 years old (3rd century AD). "Nearly 3,000 years" applies only to Tunisian heritage overall.
- Never leave bracketed placeholders in drafts. Look facts up or ask.
- When revising a draft, always give the full updated version so she can copy it.
