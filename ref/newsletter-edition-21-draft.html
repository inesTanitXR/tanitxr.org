🪐 Sous Dôme Festival #5, Call for 360° Short Films (Cité des sciences, Paris)

📅 Deadline: November 1, 2026, 11:59 pm GMT+1

📍 Planetarium of the Cité des sciences et de l'industrie, Paris; festival April 2 to 4, 2027

👥 Artists or producers from any country, fulldome shorts of 3 to 9 minutes released after January 1, 2023, up to three works each; VR not accepted. Free

✏️ Selected films screen in 35-minute dome sessions with a Jury Award and an Audience Award, get a fixed fee of up to EUR 300 and may tour partner planetariums.

👉 https://www.cite-sciences.fr/en/planetarium/translate-to-anglais-sous-dome-festival/sous-dome-festival-5-call-for-360-short-films

🥽 XR & IMMERSIVE

👓 Learn AR with SPECS, Devpost Hackathon with Snap

📅 Deadline: November 9, 2026, 5:00 pm ET

📍 Online; project review November 5, 12 pm ET, recorded

👥 Open worldwide (US-sanctioned countries excluded), adults, teams and organisations. Free

✏️ Design a new AR Lens for SPECS glasses and let a coding agent build it in Lens Studio; no glasses needed. Submit a public repo, a short description and a YouTube demo under 3 minutes. Prizes USD 1,250, 750 and 500.

👉 https://learn-specs.devpost.com/

🥽 Realities in Transition 2, Open Call #4: Relational Realities with Beta Festival

📅 Deadline: November 10, 2026, 23:59 CEST

📍 Hybrid, March to August 2027, with two months in Dublin, Ireland (May to June)

👥 XR artists and collectives, no nationality limit listed; two months in Dublin required. Free

✏️ Hybrid XR residency on relationships between human, artificial and more-than-human intelligence. Three artists each receive EUR 15,000 that covers fee, travel, accommodation and subsistence, with a showcase at Beta Festival in November 2027.

👉 https://www.realities-in-transition.eu/rit-2-open-call-4/

🌀 Athens Digital Arts Festival 2027, "It's Alive..." Open Call (AR/VR/XR)

📅 Deadline: November 10, 2026 (no time zone stated)

📍 Athens, Greece; dates to be announced

👥 Artists worldwide. Free

✏️ Greece's digital arts festival takes AR/VR/XR, games, web art and installations. Unfinished VR projects can apply if the design is complete.

👉 https://2027.adaf.gr/ar-vr-xr/

🎞 Maryland Film Festival 2027, XR and Interactive

📅 Deadline: November 20, 2026 (late January 8, extended February 5, 2027)

📍 Baltimore, April 7 to 11, 2027

👥 Makers anywhere. USD 25 to 40

✏️ A category for VR, AR, game, interactive and installation work at Baltimore's main film festival. Maryland premiere required.

👉 https://filmfreeway.com/TheMarylandFilmFestival

🥽 AGORA XR Lab 2027, Thessaloniki International Documentary Festival

📅 Deadline: November 25, 2026 (extended; no time zone stated)

📍 Online in mid January, then Thessaloniki, Greece, March 6 to 11, 2027

👥 XR teams from eligible countries, Tunisia included. No fee stated

✏️ A development lab for XR projects at any stage, with an online workshop and a week in Thessaloniki. Five nights' accommodation and part of the travel are covered.

👉 https://filmfestival.gr/en/tidf/tidf-agora/agora-xr-lab/

🎨 INTERACTIVE & MEDIA ART

💡 Code:ART 2027, Palo Alto

📅 Deadline: November 16, 2026, 11:59 pm PT

📍 Palo Alto, California; festival October 13 to 16, 2027

👥 Artists anywhere, 18+. Free

👉 https://www.schlosslichtspiele.info/en/
