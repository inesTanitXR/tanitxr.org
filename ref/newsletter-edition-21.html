🌐 Art, XR & Impact Opportunities, Edition 21

Hi everyone 👋

This edition has deadlines from now to early December, with a section at the end for Tunisians and North Africans.

A lot more opportunities, including ones that close later, are on tanitxr.org/opportunities, and that page is updated every day.

🔥 HIGHLIGHTS

  • 👓 Learn AR with SPECS, an online AR hackathon with Snap, USD 2,500 in prizes, free. Deadline Nov 9
  • 🎥 Monte Carlo VR Movies Festival, EUR 5,000 for the best 360° short. Deadline Nov 30
  • 🌀 Athens Digital Arts Festival, AR/VR/XR open call, free. Deadline Nov 10
  • 🎨 CURRENTS 2027 in Santa Fe, VR, AR and MR category. Deadline Dec 8

💰 GRANTS, PRIZES & PAID WORK

👓 Learn AR with SPECS, Devpost Hackathon with Snap

📅 Deadline: November 9, 2026, 5 pm EST

📍 Online

👥 Adults, teams and organisations worldwide (US-sanctioned countries excluded). Free, no glasses needed

✏️ Design an AR Lens for SPECS glasses and build it in Lens Studio with a coding agent. Submit a public repo, a short description and a demo video under 3 minutes. Prizes USD 1,250, 750 and 500.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/learn-ar-with-specs-devpost-hackathon-with-snap/?ref=nl21

🚇 Metro Art in Transit Open Call (Washington, DC area)

📅 Deadline: rolling

📍 DC, Maryland and Virginia, across the Metro system

👥 Artists 18 and over living in DC, Maryland or Virginia, any career stage. Free

✏️ Metro is building a pool of artists for temporary public art: construction-wall prints, 20-second digital screen pieces, station installations and community events. Selected artists quote a fee and are paid from a project budget.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/metro-art-in-transit-open-call-for-artwork-washington-dc-are/?ref=nl21

🏆 AWARDS

👩‍💻 EQUALS in Tech Awards 2027

📅 Deadline: November 7, 2026, 23:59 CET

📍 Any country, apply online

👥 Running tech projects that help women and girls, any sector. Free

✏️ Global recognition, no cash, for projects in emerging tech, digital skills and safe online spaces for women and girls.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/equals-in-tech-awards-2027/?ref=nl21

🎤 SHOW YOUR WORK: FESTIVALS & EXHIBITIONS

🌀 Athens Digital Arts Festival 2027, "It's Alive..." Open Call (AR/VR/XR category)

📅 Deadline: November 10, 2026 (no time zone stated)

📍 Athens, Greece; dates to be announced

👥 Artists worldwide. No fee listed

✏️ Greece's digital arts festival takes AR/VR/XR, games, web art and installations. Unfinished VR projects can apply if the design is complete.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/athens-digital-arts-festival-2027-it-s-alive-open-call-ar-vr/?ref=nl21

🎥 Monte Carlo VR Movies Festival 2027, 360° Short Films

📅 Deadline: November 30, 2026

📍 Monaco, screenings in June 2027

👥 Any country, 18 and over; 360° video up to 20 minutes, at least 4K. The fee is not stated on the festival site

✏️ Best 360° Short Film receives EUR 5,000 toward a new film. Entries by FilmFreeway, Vimeo or YouTube.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/monte-carlo-vr-movies-festival-2027-360-short-films/?ref=nl21

🎬 Raindance Film Festival 2027, Immersive

📅 Deadline: early December 7, 2026 (GBP 38) · regular January 18, 2027 (GBP 49) · late March 8, 2027 (GBP 66)

📍 London; entries through FilmFreeway

👥 Open to all. Raindance Create members get one free entry a year

✏️ Immersive section for VR, XR and social VR worlds. Results May 16, 2027.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/raindance-film-festival-2027-immersive/?ref=nl21

🎨 CURRENTS 2027 Art & Technology Festival, Open Call

📅 Deadline: December 8, 2026, 11:59 pm MST

📍 Santa Fe, New Mexico, June 11 to 20, 2027

👥 Artists worldwide; international shipping may not be covered. USD 30 for one entry, USD 25 each for more; hardship waivers on request

✏️ New media festival with a category for VR, AR and mixed reality, plus installation, audio, robotics, performance and video.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/currents-2027-art-technology-festival-open-call/?ref=nl21

📄 PAPERS & CONFERENCES

🖼️ DH2027, Digital Humanities Conference: Digital Art(s) Track (Galway and online)

📅 Deadline: November 20, 2026, 11:59 pm AoE

📍 Galway, Ireland, and online, June 28 to July 3, 2027

👥 Artists, practitioners and researchers worldwide. Free to submit

✏️ The Digital Art(s) track takes installations, interactive and generative works and critical making. Cultural heritage is one of the conference themes, and remote presentation is planned.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/dh2027-digital-humanities-conference-digital-art-s-track-gal/?ref=nl21

🏠 RESIDENCIES

⚡ Schaufler Residency@TU Dresden 2027: Energy Cultures in Transition

📅 Deadline: November 9, 2026

📍 TU Dresden, Germany, six months from April 2027

👥 Artists and collectives in media art, performance and related fields, any country. You must live in Dresden for the six months. Free; one PDF by email

✏️ EUR 3,000 net a month, up to EUR 10,000 for production, a campus studio and a solo show in autumn 2027. The call depends on final funding approval.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/schaufler-residency-tu-dresden-2027-energy-cultures-in-trans/?ref=nl21

🏛️ Rome Prize 2027-28, American Academy in Rome

📅 Deadline: November 15, 2026 (late window; the early deadline was Nov 1)

📍 Rome, Italy, from September 2027

👥 US citizens (some fields also open to 3-year US residents). Late fee USD 80, or USD 90 for two or more applications

✏️ A stipend of USD 30,000 or USD 16,000 with housing, a workspace and meals in Rome. Fields include visual arts, design and historic preservation.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/rome-prize-2027-28-american-academy-in-rome/?ref=nl21

🌳 Bernheim Forest Artist in Residence 2027 (Kentucky)

📅 Deadline: November 30, 2026, midnight EST

📍 Clermont, Kentucky, six to eight weeks in 2027

👥 Visual and mixed media artists, US and international (international artists arrange their own visa; travel not listed as covered). No application fee listed

✏️ Four residencies with a USD 2,500 stipend, housing and a studio, in exchange for a site-inspired work. One of the four is for an artist working on environmental issues and climate change.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/bernheim-forest-artist-in-residence-2027-kentucky/?ref=nl21

🎨 Bogliasco Foundation Fellowship, Fall 2027

📅 Deadline: December 1, 2026 (Spring 2028 round: March 11, 2027)

📍 In person, Bogliasco Study Center on the Ligurian coast near Genoa, Italy, one semester

👥 Artists and scholars in the arts and humanities, any nationality; conversational English needed. USD 30 until a week before the deadline, then USD 45

✏️ Full room and board and a work space. Travel, materials and personal expenses are yours.

👉 https://tanitxr.org/go/bogliasco-foundation-fellowship-fall-2027/?ref=nl21
