## 1. Can she go? Yes.

The GW calendar tags almost every Corcoran event "GW Community". That is a calendar category, not a door policy. Evidence:

- The Corcoran's own page says: "Our public galleries and the Luther W. Brady Art Gallery are open to the public Wednesday to Saturday, 1 to 5 p.m."

- Theatre and dance publish a general-admission price ($20, students and seniors $10), which only exists for outside guests.
- The Research + Practice event page itself carries both tags, "GW Community" and "General Public".

Rule for the future: ignore the GW calendar tag, open the Eventbrite link, register with ines@tanitxr.org. Enter the Flagg Building by the New York Avenue door.

## 4. Collaboration angles that fit what Tanit XR actually does

Lead with community and cultural exchange, objects one at a time with phones. No whole environments.

1. **Student object project with Museum Studies or Art History.** Like Unfossilizing African Art: a seminar picks Tunisian objects from our archive (or North African objects in the GW collections), researches them, writes the interpretation, and we publish them in the virtual museum with the students credited. Akpang, Anderson, van Balgooy.
2. **Scanning workshop.** One afternoon, phones only, in the Flagg Building or at the GW Museum: students scan objects the museum or the class chooses, Mark Jeffcock's method, the models go online the same week. Marigny, Wetenhall.
3. **Guest lectures.** Ines in a Museum Studies or Interaction Design class; Laura Harrison for Cline's archaeology students or an IMES brown bag. Already doing this format on Oct 27 for BUS 3835.
4. **Small exhibition from the archive.** Gallery 102 (801 22nd St NW, Smith Hall of Art) is the student and small-show space. Printed and AR, objects plus their scanners' stories, Tunisia and the volunteers on four continents.
5. **Exhibition Design or Interaction Design studio brief.** A semester project: how do you display a 3D scanned Punic stela in a room, on a phone, in a headset. Students get a portfolio piece, we get prototypes.
6. **IMES co-sponsorship on the Oct 22 embassy evening**, name on the page, students invited. Already in Draft 3.
7. **Spring 2027 event at GW** with IMES and Museum Studies, which is what Shana was already asking the Corcoran about. Shape it as number 1 or 2 ending in a public evening.
