## Smithsonian: how we get on their stage (added 2026-10-06)

There is no open call. Each museum's public programs or education staff books outside speakers around an exhibition, a festival or a series, and pays an honorarium (typical museum range $250 to $1,000 for a talk or workshop; Smithsonian's own figures [CHECK]). The way in is always a partner or a staff member. Ranked by how real it is for Ines:

1. **DC Francophonie Cultural Festival, every March, hosted in part at the National Museum of African Art (NMAfA).** The festival is programmed by the francophone embassies, and Tunisia is a member. The embassy relationship from Oct 22 is the door: ask Mr. Ezzaier, in the thank-you note after the 22nd, whether the embassy would propose a Tanit XR talk or a phone-scanning workshop in French for the March 2027 festival. That puts Ines on a Smithsonian stage with the embassy as sponsor. Proposals are usually settled by January.
2. **Smithsonian Artist Research Fellowship (SARF), $5,000 for a one-month residency between July 2027 and May 2028.** The nominator must be Smithsonian RESEARCH staff (a curator) who also advises the project; administrators like the Arts and Industries director cannot nominate. Verified from the Smithsonian research-staff directory (fellowships.si.edu/SORS): Kevin Dumouchelle, Curator, National Museum of African Art, DumouchelleK@si.edu; Marina Isgro, Curator of Media and Performance Art, Hirshhorn, IsgroM@si.edu; Massumeh Farhad, Curator of Persian, Arab and Turkish Art, National Museum of Asian Art, FarhaMa@si.edu; Simon Rettig, Islamic World, RettigS@si.edu; Saisha Grayson, Time-Based Media, SAAM, GraysonS@si.edu. Nomination by Oct 15, 2026, application Jan 28, 2027. Drafts to Dumouchelle and Isgro are in drafts-2026-10-06-smithsonian-and-honorarium.md.
3. **Smithsonian Associates** (the Smithsonian's public lecture and class program, hundreds of evening talks and studio classes a year, outside presenters, paid). No proposal form; write to CustomerService@SmithsonianAssociates.org asking to be routed to the program director, with a one-paragraph pitch: an evening talk "Tunisia in 3D: a volunteer community scanning heritage with phones" and a hands-on studio class "Scan an object with your phone". Phone 202-633-3030, weekdays 10 to 3.
4. **Smithsonian 3D Program (Digitization Program Office; Vince Rossi, Senior 3D Program Officer).** Peers, not a stage: their own page says they want an open-source community around 3D. Propose a Tanit XR x Smithsonian 3D conversation (online or at their office) and a Tanit XR volunteer session using Smithsonian open-access 3D objects. This is how the staff nominator for SARF 2027 could appear.
5. **Smithsonian Cultural Rescue Initiative (SCRI), culturalrescue@si.edu.** Heritage threatened by disasters. Neapolis exposed by Storm Harry and captured within days is exactly their subject. Offer a short case talk for their training network.
6. **Hirshhorn ARTLAB** (teen digital media lab) hires teaching artists and mentors for paid sessions; a phone-scanning session fits.
7. **National Museum of Asian Art** holds Islamic art and is building a VR Peacock Room for 2027 with Toppan; an artist talk on XR and heritage fits their public programs. Contact through asiatours@si.edu to be routed.
8. **Smithsonian Folklife Festival 2027** (60th anniversary; the Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage programs it, with national partners and embassy funding for country programs). Long shot and long lead; mention it to the embassy only if they ask what else is possible.
9. **Cultural Heritage Forward** (State Department + Smithsonian exchange, NMAfA is a hub): the 2024 cohort countries did not include Tunisia, but Nigeria was eligible. Pass the call to the Unique Mappers Network when the next cohort opens (Global@si.edu).
