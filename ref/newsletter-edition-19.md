## ⏳ CLOSING THIS WEEK

### 🏺 UNESCO International Youth Forum on Creativity and Heritage along the Silk Roads

📅 Deadline: September 27, 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (11:59 am ET)
📍 Changsha and Nanjing, China, November 15–20, 2026
👥 Ages 18–32: storytellers, filmmakers, heritage practitioners
✏️ Theme: youth-led creative heritage expression in the age of AI. Apply with a form and a video portfolio. The call does not say which costs are covered, so ask before you plan travel.
👉 https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/call-applications-2026-international-youth-forum-creativity-and-heritage-along-silk-roads

### 🕶 ReMix the Future 2026: Play Attention!

📅 Deadline: September 30, 2026 (invitations go out on a rolling basis, so apply early)
📍 ASU MIX Center, Mesa, Arizona, October 15–18, 2026
👥 Artists, designers and technologists 18+, local or from anywhere. Free to apply; travel is yours
✏️ Four-day spatial design jam in a four-storey 360° projection studio with 55-channel spatial audio and an LED virtual production stage. Immersive Experience, Interactive Installation and XR Design tracks; prize pool valued at $3,000.
👉 https://tech.asu.edu/remixthefuture

## 💰 GRANTS & FUNDING

### 💸 Voqal Partners Fellowship 2027

📅 Deadline: October 31, 2026, 11:59 pm ET
📍 Remote, April–September 2027, with travel covered to three in-person convenings
👥 Anyone 18+ based in the United States; no organization, finished product or business plan required
✏️ $30,000 unrestricted, plus a $1,000 healthcare stipend and a childcare stipend for fellows with dependents, for early-stage social justice ideas. Free to apply.
👉 https://voqalpartners.org/fellowship/

### 🎭 Goethe-Institut International Coproduction Fund, Round 2

📅 Deadline: October 12, 2026, 23:59 CET
📍 A coproduction with a partner in Germany, running January–December 2027
👥 Organisations based outside Germany. Contact your local Goethe-Institut before applying. Free
✏️ EUR 15,000 to 30,000, up to 75% of production costs, for new music, theatre, dance or performance work. Digital and hybrid projects qualify.
👉 https://www.goethe.de/en/kul/foe/int.html

## 🎨 IMMERSIVE ART & FILM

### 🌍 Earth Partner Prize 2026

📅 Deadline: October 7, 2026, 23:59 CEST
📍 Online entry
👥 Creatives aged 14 to 30, any nationality, any medium including new media, film and apps. Free
✏️ Climate and environment prize. Eight finalists win $10,000, $5,000 or $2,000, and 20 more get honourable mentions. Enter with up to 10 images or videos and a written reflection.
👉 https://earthpartner.com/earth-partner-prize/

### 🕹 Slamdance 2027: DIG (Digital Interactive Gallery)

📅 Extended deadline: October 6, 2026, 11:59 pm PST ($90)
📍 Los Angeles, February 18–24, 2027
👥 VR/AR, interactive documentaries, games, live performance, AI-driven work, installations
✏️ Selected artists attend and handle their own setup and materials. Notifications December 17.
👉 https://slamdance.com/festival-submit/

### 🏔 Djerassi Resident Artists Program 2027

📅 Deadline: October 9, 2026
📍 Santa Cruz Mountains, California, one month
👥 National and international artists; media arts and technology-driven work is a listed discipline
✏️ No cost to artists. Five cohorts between February and November 2027; the June 22 – July 27 cohort is Leonardo@Djerassi, for art and science.
👉 https://djerassi.org/apply/2027residency/

### 🏆 Aesthetica Art Prize 2026

📅 Deadline: October 11, 2026 (£25) · late entries until October 18 (£35)
📍 Online entry; shortlist exhibited autumn 2027
👥 Any discipline, any level. Emerging Prize for students and graduates of the last three years
✏️ £10,000 Main Prize, £1,000 Emerging Prize. Two works per entry; work from the last three years, previously exhibited work accepted.
👉 https://aestheticamagazine.com/artprize/submit/

### 🪐 Dome Fest West 2027

📅 Regular deadline: October 16, 2026 · late December 4 · final January 8, 2027
📍 Fiske Planetarium, Boulder, Colorado, April 21–25, 2027
👥 Fulldome films, live dome experiences, interactive dome work completed after January 1, 2025
✏️ The only festival just for fulldome and immersive cinema. Lineup announced February 9. Fees on FilmFreeway.
👉 https://domefestwest.com/

## 🎤 SPEAK & BUILD

### 🎤 ACM CHI 2027: Workshops, Judge One or Propose One

📅 Juror applications: September 28, 2026 · workshop proposals: October 1, 2026
📍 Pittsburgh, May 10–14, 2027
👥 Researchers, educators and practitioners with conference or reviewing experience. Free both ways
✏️ To judge: email your name, affiliation, CV and a 150-word statement to workshops@chi2027.acm.org, about eight hours of reviewing in October and November. To organize: up to six pages excluding references, via PCS.
👉 https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/workshops/

### 🎤 ACM CHI 2027: Call for Meet-ups

📅 Deadline: October 1, 2026, AoE
📍 Pittsburgh, May 10–14, 2027
👥 Open to all, no academic affiliation needed. Free to submit; two or more organizers from different organizations preferred
✏️ The informal, participant-driven side of CHI: three-page proposal plus two pages of supplementary material. One organizer has to register and attend in person.
👉 https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/meet-ups/

### 🎤 iLRN 2027: Immersive Learning Research Network

📅 Academic Stream: October 4, 2026 · practitioner iLEAD stream: January 12, 2027
📍 Online June 11–13, 2027; in person June 26–29, Daegu, Korea
👥 Researchers, educators, designers, practitioners. No submission fee
✏️ Theme "XR + AI Collaboration". Full and short papers, or a 4–7 page extended abstract for a poster. Practitioners: the iLEAD stream takes a 2–5 page abstract for a talk, workshop or demo.
👉 https://www.immersivelrn.org/ilrn2027/call-for-proposals/

### 💻 Women Who Code Summit 2026

📅 October 6–8, 2026; speaker applications reviewed on a rolling basis
📍 New York, or online
👥 Open to all. Virtual Pass free; in-person from $499
✏️ The free Virtual Pass covers the leadership and AI upskilling workshops. There is a virtual-only speaking option.
👉 https://events.womenwhocode.com/2026/home

### 💻 Technica 2026

📅 Hackathon October 17–18, 2026; registration open, no closing date published
📍 College Park, Maryland, or online
👥 All genders welcome, built for underrepresented genders in tech. No coding experience needed
✏️ The University of Maryland's hackathon, 500+ hackers last year. Beginner, General, Research and Startup tracks.
👉 https://gotechnica.org/

## 🇹🇳 FOR TUNISIANS & NORTH AFRICANS

### 🗼 Institut français x Cité internationale des arts: Paris Residencies 2027–2028

📅 Deadline: October 8, 2026, 23:59 Paris time
📍 Paris, between April 2027 and April 2028
👥 Artists and cultural professionals living outside France
✏️ Residencies at the Cité internationale des arts for research and creative projects. Apply on the Institut français eMundus platform.
👉 https://ifprog.emundus.fr/

### 🏛 Wiki Loves Monuments 2026 in Tunisia

📅 Upload window: October 1–31, 2026
📍 Online; photos of any of Tunisia's 2,600+ listed historic and cultural sites
👥 Open to all. Free
✏️ Photos go on Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0. Prizes of 700, 400 and 250 TND plus a 250 TND special prize; the top 10 go on to the international round. Photos can be taken any time, but must be uploaded in October.
👉 https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2026_in_Tunisia

### ✍️ Africa No Filter, Kekere Storytellers Fund

📅 Open until December 31, 2026, or until 12–14 storytellers are chosen, so apply early
📍 Remote
👥 African storytellers under 35, in Africa or the diaspora, with two years of experience and 5,000+ followers. English or French. Free
✏️ Grants of $500 to $3,000 for projects that challenge stereotypes about Africa. You join the African Narrative Collective to apply. Film projects and equipment are not funded.
👉 https://africanofilter.org/what-we-do/community/kekere-storytellers-fund/
