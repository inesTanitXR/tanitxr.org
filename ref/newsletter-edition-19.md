## ⏳ CLOSING THIS WEEK

### 🏺 UNESCO International Youth Forum on Creativity and Heritage along the Silk Roads

📅 Deadline: September 27, 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (11:59 am ET)
📍 Changsha and Nanjing, China, November 15–20, 2026
👥 Ages 18–32: storytellers, filmmakers, heritage practitioners
✏️ Theme: youth-led creative heritage expression in the age of AI. Apply with a form and a video portfolio. The call does not say which costs are covered, so ask before you plan travel.
👉 https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/call-applications-2026-international-youth-forum-creativity-and-heritage-along-silk-roads

### 🕶 ReMix the Future 2026: Play Attention!

📅 Deadline: September 30, 2026 (invitations go out on a rolling basis, so apply early)
📍 ASU MIX Center, Mesa, Arizona, October 15–18, 2026
👥 Artists, designers and technologists 18+, local or from anywhere. Free to apply; travel is yours
✏️ Four-day spatial design jam in a four-storey 360° projection studio with 55-channel spatial audio and an LED virtual production stage. Immersive Experience, Interactive Installation and XR Design tracks; prize pool valued at $3,000.
👉 https://tech.asu.edu/remixthefuture

## 🏆 AWARDS

### 🌍 Earth Partner Prize 2026

📅 Deadline: October 7, 2026, 23:59 CEST
📍 Online entry
👥 Creatives aged 14 to 30, any nationality, any medium including new media, film and apps. Free
✏️ Climate and environment prize. Eight finalists win $10,000, $5,000 or $2,000, and 20 more get honourable mentions. Enter with up to 10 images or videos and a written reflection.
👉 https://earthpartner.com/earth-partner-prize/

### 🏆 Aesthetica Art Prize 2026

📅 Deadline: October 11, 2026 (£25) · late entries until October 18 (£35)
📍 Online entry; shortlist exhibited autumn 2027
👥 Any discipline, any level. Emerging Prize for students and graduates of the last three years
✏️ £10,000 Main Prize, £1,000 Emerging Prize. Two works per entry; work from the last three years, previously exhibited work accepted.
👉 https://aestheticamagazine.com/artprize/submit/

### 🏆 The 31st Webby Awards, incl. Immersive and AI

📅 Early entry deadline: October 30, 2026 (cheapest rate; later deadlines cost more)
📍 Online entry
👥 Anyone with work on the internet: sites, apps, video, social, podcasts, games, creators, AI and immersive
✏️ Paid entry: Student $105, discounted categories $255, Creator single $395, General single $525. Immersive categories include Best Use of Augmented Reality, Best VR Headset Experience and Best Immersive Technology Innovation.
👉 https://www.webbyawards.com/eligibility-and-guidelines/

## 🎤 SPEAK & BUILD

### 🎤 ACM CHI 2027: Four Ways In

📅 Workshop jurors: September 28, 2026 · workshop proposals and meet-ups: October 1, 2026, AoE · Digital Experience Competition: December 4, 2026, AoE
📍 Pittsburgh, May 10–14, 2027 (the competition is built remotely)
👥 Open to all, no academic affiliation needed for meet-ups or the competition. Free to submit, all four
✏️ Judge: email your CV and a 150-word statement to workshops@chi2027.acm.org, about eight hours of reviewing in October and November. Propose a workshop (up to six pages) or a meet-up (three pages, two organizers from different organizations preferred); one organizer has to attend in person. Or build an open-source app that improves the attendee experience and demo it at CHI.
👉 https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/workshops/ · https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/meet-ups/ · https://chi2027.acm.org/digital-experience-competition/

### 🎤 iLRN 2027: Immersive Learning Research Network

📅 Academic Stream: October 4, 2026 · practitioner iLEAD stream: January 12, 2027
📍 Online June 11–13, 2027; in person June 26–29, Daegu, Korea
👥 Researchers, educators, designers, practitioners. No submission fee
✏️ Theme "XR + AI Collaboration". Full and short papers, or a 4–7 page extended abstract for a poster. Practitioners: the iLEAD stream takes a 2–5 page abstract for a talk, workshop or demo.
👉 https://www.immersivelrn.org/ilrn2027/call-for-proposals/

### 💻 Women Who Code Summit 2026

📅 October 6–8, 2026; speaker applications reviewed on a rolling basis
📍 New York, or online
👥 Open to all. Virtual Pass free; in-person from $499
✏️ The free Virtual Pass covers the leadership and AI upskilling workshops. There is a virtual-only speaking option.
👉 https://events.womenwhocode.com/2026/home

### 💻 Technica 2026

📅 Hackathon October 17–18, 2026; registration open, no closing date published
📍 College Park, Maryland, or online
👥 All genders welcome, built for underrepresented genders in tech. No coding experience needed
✏️ The University of Maryland's hackathon, 500+ hackers last year. Beginner, General, Research and Startup tracks.
👉 https://gotechnica.org/
