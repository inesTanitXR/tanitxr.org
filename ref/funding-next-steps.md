# Funding: concrete next steps

Researched 2026-09-25 (every page checked that day; "unverified" means the official page would not open
and the detail comes from a listing). Tanit XR applies as a project of Florida Community Innovation
(FCI), a U.S. 501(c)(3), which matters: some funds want the sponsor as the applicant, some want a
Tunisian partner, and a few cannot take a U.S. entity at all.

## Today, in an hour, no money needed

1. **Email the U.S. Embassy in Tunis** at PASTunisLargeGrants@state.gov. Three paragraphs: who we are, the
   Neapolis scan after Storm Harry (the only 3D record), the DC event on 22 October, and a request to be
   told when the 2027 Ambassadors Fund for Cultural Preservation concept-note call opens (it opened late
   January the last two years, notes due end of March; $25k to $250k; Tunisian and U.S. NGOs eligible;
   AFCP funded El Jem digitisation before). Best fit on the list.
   https://tn.usembassy.gov/call-for-u-s-ambassadors-fund-for-cultural-preservation-afcp-freedom-250-special-edition/
2. **Submit to the Awesome Foundation, Washington DC chapter** ($1,000, no strings, monthly, anyone can apply).
   Tie it to the 22 October event: "a scanning evening for the Tunisian diaspora in DC".
   https://www.awesomefoundation.org/en/submissions/new
3. **Email Gerda Henkel Foundation, Patrimonies programme** (elsper@gerda-henkel-stiftung.de and
   kothe@gerda-henkel-stiftung.de): one page on documenting Neapolis and the storm-exposed coast, ask for a
   preliminary call. Rolling, no deadline, funds documentation of heritage endangered by natural disasters
   in Africa; wants an institution in the region as partner (the INP or a Tunisian association).
   https://www.gerda-henkel-stiftung.de/en/patrimonies_en
4. **Ask FCI three things in one email**: (a) claim their Benevity Causes profile and add "Tanit XR" as a
   project, so employees at big companies can give with matching; (b) enrol Tanit XR in Candid's free
   fiscally-sponsored-project profile (a GuideStar seal of our own); (c) whether they would host us on
   Open Collective, which gives a public ledger and recurring gifts.
   https://causeshelp.benevity.org/hc/en-us/articles/360001159046-What-are-projects
   https://learning.candid.org/fiscally-sponsored-projects-can-claim-a-candid-profile/457881
   https://docs.opencollective.com/help/fiscal-hosts/become-a-fiscal-host
5. **Put the tour link in your email signature**: tanitxr.org/explore/?tour=1 (four minutes, ends on Donate),
   and the impact page tanitxr.org/impact/ under it.
6. **Email Sketchfab's community team** for a Cultural Heritage Spotlight post (no grant, but their reach).
   https://sketchfab.com/museums

## This week

7. **Wikimedia Foundation Rapid Fund**, $500 to $5,000, cycle 3 closes **1 November 2026**. Needs a named
   Wikimedia contributor on the team and Wikimedia output: Commons only takes STL for 3D, so pitch photos,
   STL derivatives and Wikipedia articles on Neapolis, the Zaghouan temple and the Bachia madrasa in
   Arabic, French and English. Create the Fluxx account now. https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Grants:Project/Rapid
8. **Cultural Emergency Response** (formerly Prince Claus emergency programme): year-round, Tunisia
   eligible, documentary-heritage grants around €15k. Describe the Neapolis exposure through
   "Apply for emergency support" on https://www.culturalemergency.org (their form page errored; unverified).
9. **Register on the British Council Cultural Protection Fund platform** and line up a Tunisian registered
   partner (INP, or an association). 2026 round is closed; Tunisia is a target country and climate damage
   is a named theme; £100k to £500k awards; be ready if a 2027 round is announced.
   https://culturalprotectionfund.grantplatform.com/
10. **Set two prices** so the site can ask: a featured listing on the Opportunities board (the board has
    90 open calls and its own newsletter; the per-listing clicks are the rate card) and "adopt an object"
    tiers ($5 a month, name on the label). Never volunteer labour.
