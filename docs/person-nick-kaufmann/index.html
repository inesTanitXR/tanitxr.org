Nick Kaufmann

Home  ›  Our People  ›  Nick Kaufmann
Photogrammetry, Grants and Opportunities

Nick Kaufmann is a community builder and multidisciplinary practitioner working across digital and physical space. He facilitates events, workshops and partnerships that help both neighborhood-based and online communities engage with space and place, using a range of traditional and emerging media and technology. He leads partnerships and ecosystem at inCitu, a New York XR startup focused on community engagement with the built environment, and draws on a background in social science, games and XR, urban placemaking, and civic technology. He is a former Fulbright Fellow, WEF Global Shaper, Code for America Brigade captain, Mozilla Open Leader, and serial TEDx organizer.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
