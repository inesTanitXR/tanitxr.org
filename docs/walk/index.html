How these are made

You circle it, slowly

That is the whole technique. Walk a full circle around the object with your phone, then again higher, then again lower, overlapping every shot with the last. We use Scaniverse, which is free, and we capture a mesh rather than a splat.

Scanning heritage is not only a technique. Our guide covers permission and care as well as method.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
