Illustrator and Designer

Alyssa George is an illustrator and designer who provides 2D and 3D design support to Tanit XR. She enjoys playing with vibrant colour and texture to create striking visual narratives. She holds a BFA in Animation and Digital Modeling from the University of South Florida and hopes to use it to uplift underrepresented stories. She modelled the ceramic plate in the Collection and drew the illustration of the Tanit XR mission the community shared in July 2026.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
