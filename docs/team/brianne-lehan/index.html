Brianne Lehan

Home  ›  Our People  ›  Brianne Lehan
Strategy & Creative Support

Brianne Lehan is a multimedia storyteller, photographer, and videographer based in Central Florida whose work spans documentary, commercial, conservation, and community-focused content. She leads strategic communications and produces photo/video content for major social platforms and campaigns, combining creative vision with technical skill to help shape compelling narratives. Brianne has a passion for environmental and science communication, uplifting community voices, and visual storytelling that drives impact. In her work with Tanit XR she has participated in strategy meetings, contributed to video editing, and supported grant writing and project development, helping strengthen our communications and creative output.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
