Bird of Prey Statue – Roman Villas of Carthage

الرئيسية  ›  الأرشيف  ›  Bird of Prey Statue – Roman Villas of Carthage
CarthageRoman Villas of Carthage

This weathered stone sculpture, found among the Roman villas of Carthage, depicts a bird of prey with folded wings and a curved beak — likely an eagle. In Roman culture, the eagle was a powerful symbol of strength, divinity, and the authority of the empire. Though time has softened many of the details, the form is still recognizable.
