استخدام Scaniverse لصون التراث

TanitXR creates detailed 3D scans of historical and archaeological sites, landmarks, pottery, architecture, and cultural artifacts for an open, global digital heritage archive. But heritage is never just an object, whether digital or physical. It belongs to living communities, it carries meaning and memory, and it is often protected by law and by custom. This guide covers how to capture a great scan, and how to do it ethically: with consent, care, and respect for the people whose heritage it is.

Our practice follows recognized standards in archaeology and digital heritage, including the London Charter and the Seville Principles for computer-based visualization of cultural heritage, UNESCO's 2003 Convention for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage, and the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance. You don't need to memorize these, but know that the TanitXR Scanning Guide incorporates these standards into field practice.

Our Ethical Commitments

As a TanitXR Heritage Scout, you are part of a global community that is dedicated to upholding the highest standards in ethical digital heritage. Our core pillars are:

  • People and communities first. We document heritage with communities, not just about them. The people who hold, use, and remember a place or object have the first and final say in how it is documented and shared.
  • Consent where it counts. For publicly visible heritage, building exteriors, monuments, facades, ruins, and public art, public visibility is generally a sound basis to document, subject to local rules and any permit requirements. For living or community heritage we seek free, prior, and informed consent from the relevant owners, caretakers, or cultural authorities first. This process requires the involvement of the TanitXR Leadership Team.
  • المجتمع authority and data sovereignty. Communities decide whether their heritage is scanned, what is captured, and whether the result is shared openly, shared conditionally, or kept restricted. This authority continues after the scan is made.
  • Do no harm. We do not scan sacred, ceremonial, funerary, or human remains, private interiors, or objects of contested or unclear ownership without explicit authorization from the right people.
  • Transparency and authenticity. We always document where a scan came from, and how it was made.
  • Reciprocity and benefit. Documentation should benefit the community whose heritage it is, for example, by offering them a copy of the scan and crediting the knowledge holders who made it possible.
  • Legal compliance. We respect national and local heritage laws, protected-site designations, and permit requirements. When a permit is required, we obtain it before scanning.

Digitizing Tangible Heritage (القطع and الموقعs)

Note: Many cultural heritage sites and points of interest are protected and managed by governmental organizations. To digitize sites within these areas, formal permission is required.

TanitXR is digitizing heritage sites or archaeological features that are plainly visible in public places. This includes what you can see from streets and public plazas.

لا تعرف من أين تبدأ؟ ابحث عن أشياء وأماكن وتفاصيل تحمل معنى ثقافيًا أو تاريخيًا أو فنيًا أو مجتمعيًا. These sites are all around you!

Good things to scan include:

  • العمارة والأطلال, doors, arches, columns, facades, walls, courtyards, monuments, and the exterior of historic homes
  • القطع والآثار, pottery, tools, carvings, statues, tiles, textiles, inscriptions, and household items
  • تفاصيل صغيرة, patterns, textures, symbols, damage, repairs, maker's marks, or decorative elements
  • التراث اليومي, مخابز وورش وأسواق وأماكن تجمّع وموروثات عائلية وحدائق وفضاءات مجتمعية
  • التراث المهدد, أماكن أو أشياء يهددها المناخ أو الإهمال أو العمران أو النزاعات أو السرقة أو فقدان الذاكرة

What not to scan:

  • Shiny or reflective textures (difficult for scanner to pick up)
  • Moveable objects (like textiles or tapestries that are blowing in the wind)
  • Busy areas (pedestrian and vehicle traffic can add noise to your scan and complicate processing)

Digitizing Intangible Heritage (Stories and Practices)

Some heritage is not just a building or object. It lives in stories, songs, rituals, recipes, crafts, dances, languages, memories, and everyday practices. This is called intangible heritage.

You cannot always 3D scan intangible heritage directly, but you can document the objects, spaces, and people connected to it.

Examples:

  • وصفة خبز تقليدية ← امسح الفرن أو الأدوات أو الطاولة أو المخبزة
  • حرفة نسيج ← امسح المنسج أو زخارف النسيج أو الأدوات أو القطع الجاهزة
  • حرفة في طريق الاندثار ← امسح الأدوات والورشة والمواد والأعمال المنجزة
  • حكاية عائلية ← امسح البيت أو الفناء أو الصورة أو الشيء أو المكان المرتبط بالذكرى. Proceed with caution, as homes are private spaces and family memories are personal. تسجيل them only with documented consent, and respect any limits that families set on how their story, home, and belongings are shared.
  • مهرجان أو طقس ← امسح الزينة أو الأزياء أو الآلات أو أماكن التجمع أو الأشياء الرمزية. Proceed with caution, as working on ceremonies, sacred practices and objects may be restricted or require additional permissions.

When documenting intangible heritage, include context with your upload:

  • What is this tradition, story, or practice called?
  • Who practices it?
  • Where does it happen?
  • How did you learn about it?
  • Why does it matter?
  • Is there a story, memory, or quote that should go with the scan?

Getting Started

App: Download Scaniverse (free, by Niantic Spatial, Inc.) from the iOS App Store or Google Play.

الهدف: إنشاء نماذج ثلاثية الأبعاد مفصّلة للمعالم التاريخية والآثار والفخار والعمارة والقطع الثقافية لأرشيف رقمي عالمي للتراث.

Minimum System Requirements

iOS (iPhone / iPad)

  • iOS 16.6 or later
  • A device with the A12 Bionic chip or later:
    • iPhone XR / XS / XS Max or newer
    • iPhone SE (2nd or 3rd generation)
    • iPad (8th gen+), iPad Air (4th gen+), iPad Mini (5th gen+), iPad Pro 11" (all), iPad Pro 12.9" (3rd gen+)
  • جديدer iPhones scan faster and more accurately, but any supported device works.

Android

  • Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later
  • At least 4 GB of RAM
  • ARCore with Depth API support
  • On Android, Scaniverse builds 3D models from camera footage (photogrammetry). Scans work well but take longer to process than on modern iPhones.

First Launch: اختر "Classic"

When you first open Scaniverse, you'll see a "اختر your experience" screen with two options: جديد and Classic.

  • Select "Classic." This is the experience Tanit XR uses, it processes scans on your phone, supports Mesh mode, and lets you export FBX files directly from the app.
  • The "جديد" experience requires creating an account and is designed for teams using Niantic's cloud-based الموقعs workflow, which uses a monthly credit system. We are not using it at this time.
  • No account needed in Classic mode. If the app asks you to sign in or create an account, just tap "Skip for now", you can scan, process, and export without one.
  • If you accidentally chose "جديد," you can switch back: go to Library → Settings and select the Classic experience.

Step-by-Step Scanning Instructions

  1. Open Scaniverse (Classic experience)

    • Tap the "+" button to start a new scan.
    • اختر "Mesh" (not "Splat"), this is what we need for Tanit XR.
    • Select the size of your object:
      • قطعة صغيرة – pottery, carvings, statues
      • قطعة متوسطة – doors, columns, mosaics
      • مساحة كبيرة – facades, walls, monuments
    • The app automatically uses the best scanning method for your device, no extra setup needed on iOS or Android.

  2. Begin the Scan

    • Point the camera at the object, then tap the record button to start, no need to hold it down. Tap again when you're finished.
    • Only seeing red lines on screen? Check that the lighting is good, then point the camera directly at the object before tapping the record button. As you scan, some areas of your scene will have diagonal red lines and others will not, the red-lined areas are the parts you haven't captured yet, so keep moving until they're gone.
    • تحرّك ببطء حول الشيء أثناء تسجيل الفيديو. keep the camera slowly moving at all times; don't pause or go too fast, as the camera can get disoriented
    • التقط زوايا متعددة: دُر حوله، وانخفض، وارفع هاتفك، وهكذا.
    • تجنّب الحركات السريعة واحرص على تغطية كل الجوانب.
    • scans can be completed indoors or outdoors. The most important thing is even lighting (lack of shadows or bright lights).
    • Pedestrian traffic and/or moveable objects in the area should also be avoided. Aim to capture objects with a fixed shape.
    • تحت الشمس الساطعة، حاول المسح في ظل جزئي أو في ضوء غائم.
    • Aim for 1–3 minutes of scanning, longer scans can actually reduce quality.
    • As you scan, blurry areas on screen show where you still need coverage. Keep moving until the whole object looks sharp.
    • Android users: capture extra overlapping angles and passes. Android relies entirely on camera footage to build the 3D model, so thorough coverage and good, even light matter even more.

  3. Save Without Processing (Important!)

    • When you are done scanning, tap Area or Detail to process the scan immediately, or Save Draft to process later.
    • Note: processing your data can drain your battery, so you may want to save your captures and then process them later with your phone plugged in to a power source. This is especially true on Android, where processing takes longer than on modern iPhones.
    • We recommend processing your scan with the Detail setting, which can take several minutes.

Processing and Exporting

  1. Back at Home: Process Your Scan

    • Open the Scaniverse Library (bottom menu).
    • Tap your محفوظ scan.
    • Tap the name and give it a clear title (e.g., "Ksar Ouled Soltane – Main Door").
    • Tap "Process" and choose the Detail setting, then wait for the app to complete the 3D model.
    • Keep Scaniverse open in the foreground while it processes. On high-end Android phones this typically takes a few minutes per scan; older devices may take longer.

  2. Export the الوضعl

    • Once processed, tap "Share" > "Export الوضعl."
    • Select FBX format.
    • Keep textures enabled.
    • Name it "Scan Title_Location"

  3. Share Your الوضعl

    Upload your file, below, and then email description and other details to info@tanitxr.org

✅ نصائح لمسح متقن

  • امسح ببطء وثبات
  • Spend 1–3 minutes per scan, longer can reduce quality
  • تجنّب الأشخاص والظلال في المسح
  • Focus on texture and angles, walk around the object fully
  • Watch for blurry areas on screen and re-scan them until they sharpen
  • Natural daylight is good, but harsh sun causes glare – avoid scanning at noon
  • Charge your phone before heading out, scanning and processing use a lot of battery
  • Keep Scaniverse open while your scan is processing
