تونس وأمريكا، نتعلّم معًا: An Evening at the سفارة تونس

الرئيسية  ›  الأخبار  ›  تونس وأمريكا، نتعلّم معًا: An Evening a

بقلم Caroline Nickerson · نُشر في 2026-10-30

At “Tunisia & America: Learning Together,” volunteers showed how a smartphone scan can connect people, places, و centuries of history.

What does it feel like to explore ancient قرطاج? At the سفارة تونس in Washington, D.C., guests had a chance to find out. Through a virtual reality headset, they could see a digital replica of a carved stone from the Tophet of Salammbo و examine details a volunteer had captured in Tunisia with a smartphone.

The demonstration capped “تونس وأمريكا، نتعلّم معًا,” hosted on October 23, 2026, by the Embassy of the Republic of Tunisia in partnership with Tanit XR و Tunisian American Young Professionals (TAYP).

To begin, Chargé d’Affaires Mohamed Nejib Gorgi gave the Embassy’s official welcome, و TAYP introduced the evening. Both shared their excitement for more Tunisian-American collaborations. First Secretary Mohamed Nidhal Ezzaier, who did much of the planning with Tanit XR, welcomed the team to the Embassy.

Then, Tanit XR founder Ines Said و د. كارولين نيكرسون told the story of the volunteers و friendships behind TanitXR.

A museum begins with a phone

In her presentation, “Scans to XR: A Practical Pipeline for Cultural Heritage,” Said introduced guests to Tunisia’s Roman villas, mosaics, و amphitheaters.

Flooding و coastal erosion threaten sites across the country, و few communities can afford professional scanners.

In جانفي 2026, Storm Harry stripped sand from Tunisia’s coast near Nabeul, exposing part of the ancient city of نيابوليس. Tunisia’s national news agency reported that heritage officials moved quickly to document the finds. Tanit XR mobilized its volunteers within days.

التطوّع Youssef Lakdhar scanned the newly visible ruins, recording where the stones lay just after the storm. Researchers can return to that record as the shoreline continues to change.

Said wants people who know these places well to be able to document them with the phones they already carry.

As a core part of TanitXR’s model, volunteers learn photogrammetry in a six-week course led by Mark Jeffcock. Each week, they photograph an object from several angles, turn the overlapping images into a 3D model, و bring it back to class for feedback.

After the course, volunteers in Tunisia و beyond can go to the field. For statues و buildings, the team uses Scaniverse, which works even at sites without cell service. For smaller objects, such as pottery, volunteers often use Kiri Engine.

The original scan stays in the archive while volunteers from around the world research the object’s history و prepare a copy for public use.

التطوّع Rachel West, for example, made the model of the قرطاج stone small enough to load on a phone.

The team has documented more than 70 artifacts at over 20 locations in Tunisia alone. Tanit XR was named a 2026 Auggie Award finalist for Best Societal الأثر.

TanitXR is actively expanding to other countries, including Nigeria, و volunteers are instructed to only scan sites they are legally allowed to scan, as well as to scan other aspects of their heritage (like handicrafts) they might want to share with the world.

From digital artifacts to lasting connections

In “A Year with Tanit XR,” Nickerson showed how citizen science و civic technology come together in the museum.

Citizen science is meaningful public engagement in research – actually creating real knowledge. Civic technology is technology built for social good.

In Nickerson’s eyes, TanitXR is both a citizen science و civic technology مشروع.

People in Tunisia و beyond create a record of their own heritage with each scan. Then, working with teammates on four continents, volunteers research what the objects were used for, translate descriptions, و decide how to tell their stories to the public.

Nickerson leads partnerships و community for Tanit XR و directs Florida Community Innovation, the project’s nonprofit fiscal sponsor.

As SciStarter (the world’s largest citizen science database) founder Darlene Cavalier wrote in a message Nickerson shared during the talk, “Tanit XR is pioneering a new model for citizen science, empowering people to preserve heritage قبل it disappears.”

Part of what distinguishes TanitXR is that global volunteers come to love Tunisia و learn about the heritage by engaging deeply with the scans و building celebratory experiences.

For example, three scans from the collection became the starting point for an exhibit at MIT Reality Hack. Said, Nickerson, Elena Piech, و Anthony Ovando built it around Queen Elissa, also known as Dido, the legendary founder of قرطاج. Visitors select a burial marker, a draped statue, or a Roman column to hear Nura, the museum guide, tell part of Elissa’s story. The team spelled out names phonetically so Nura would use their Tunisian Arabic pronunciations.

Nickerson also led a citizen science workshop at Georgia Tech’s ImmerseGT. The winning team in Tanit XR’s heritage challenge built من الغموض إلى التاريخ, a proof of concept design for augmented reality that lets visitors compare possible reconstructions of a damaged artifact.

Then, at Florida Community Innovation’s CityCamp Gainesville Hack Day, participants used the scans to design a history lesson و tell a story through maps.

TanitXR, by engaging global volunteers, fosters lasting love for Tunisia.

On visits to Tunisia in November 2025 و أوت 2026, Nickerson explored the Roman amphitheater at El Jem و seaside ruins of Kerkouane with Tunisian friends. Ultimately, TanitXR is a labor of love, grounded in deep friendship. “I want everyone to have an Ines in their life!” she wrote on a slide about Said.

Nickerson hopes that every American ends up with a Tunisian best friend, just like her. In fact, the two countries signed their Treaty of Peace و Friendship in Tunis in August 1797. At the Embassy 229 years later, Nickerson urged people in both countries to keep that friendship alive by studying abroad, working together, و visiting one another.

An invitation to explore و participate

After the talks و audience questions, guests stayed to try the headsets. The stone they held in Washington is part of Tanit XR’s open 3D collection, available on a phone or computer. المتطوعون are still building the larger community museum و hope to share the emergency scanning methods they used at نيابوليس with other communities.

Many volunteers had no ties to Tunisia when they joined, Nickerson said. Now they meet every week to study its history together. Anyone with an interest in history, art, research, or technology can join them on Zoom on Thursdays at noon Eastern. No previous 3D experience is required.

Tanit XR thanks the Embassy of the Republic of Tunisia for hosting, TAYP for co-organizing the evening, و everyone who shared ideas, asked questions, و helped bring Tunisia’s heritage into the room.
