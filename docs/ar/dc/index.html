An evening on Tunisia's heritage، وكذلك technology now protecting it

With د. لورا هاريسون و د. كارولين نيكرسون, hosted by the Embassy of Tunisia with Tanit XR و TAYP. Seats are limited, so please sign up below.

Your confirmation و details for the evening come by email. Questions: info@tanitxr.org.

The evening

Three things you will come away with

1

A year of work, told by the people who did it

Over the past year, د. لورا هاريسون و د. كارولين نيكرسون have been at the centre of Tanit XR: leading its scientific programme, building partnerships with universities و institutions, running hackathons و workshops, و travelling to Tunisia this summer to work alongside Tunisian colleagues at the sites themselves. They will talk about that year, what they saw, و where it goes next. Ines Said, who founded Tanit XR, opens the evening with the story of why a Tunisian developer started scanning قرطاج with a phone, و shows the collection live.

2

The technology, up close

Tanit XR documents Tunisian heritage with photogrammetry, lidar و Gaussian splatting, much of it captured on ordinary phones by trained volunteers, و brings the results to anyone in the world through a browser, a headset or a virtual museum. The collection is already open at tanitxr.org/explore, و VR headsets will be in the room for anyone who would like to stand inside it.

3

Culture travelling both ways

Tanit XR is run by volunteers in many countries, most of whom had no connection to Tunisia when they joined. They meet every week, study Tunisian history together, و several have since travelled to Tunisia or now speak about its heritage in their own cities. That exchange, with technology as the bridge, is what the evening is about. Tunisians و friends of Tunisia equally welcome; students very welcome; no technical background needed.

The speakers

Who you will hear

د. لورا هاريسون

المسؤولة العلمية, Tanit XR

An archaeologist who specialises in digital documentation, from lidar و photogrammetry to immersive technology, she was the founding director of the Access 3D Lab at the University of South Florida, with museum experience at institutions like the Smithsonian و جديد York State Museum. She is the author of two books on archaeology و digital heritage, Associate Editor of the journal Studies in Digital Heritage, و a Commissioner on the City of Tampa's Historic Preservation Board.

د. كارولين نيكرسون

Partnerships و المجتمع, Tanit XR

Executive Director و co-founder of Florida Community Innovation, the nonprofit that serves as Tanit XR's fiscal sponsor. Her work brings together civic engagement, public interest technology و citizen science, including roles with SciStarter, CitSci و NASA-funded EMERGE مشروع. She holds a PhD in Agricultural Education و Communication from the University of Florida و a Master of Public Policy from American University.

Ines Said

المؤسِّسة, Tanit XR

A Tunisian XR artist و developer from Nabeul. Her work has been shown in the Smithsonian's FUTURES exhibition, she is a 2026 Auggie Awards finalist, one of NAAEE's 30 Under 30, و a speaker at AWE. She founded Tanit XR to put Tunisia's heritage in the hands of anyone, anywhere, و built the volunteer community that now does it. She opens the evening و shows the collection live.
