قرطاجحمامات أنطونينوس

These monumental marble fragments come from the Antonine Baths of قرطاج, one of the grandest Roman bath complexes in North Africa. The pieces preserve intricate carved decoration and traces of Latin inscriptions, which once adorned the massive structures of the baths. The Antonine Baths, built in the 2nd century CE under Emperor Antoninus Pius, symbolized both luxury and civic life in Roman قرطاج. Decorative architectural elements like these remind us of the monumental scale and artistry that made the baths a centerpiece of the city. 📌 This model is part of the Tanit XR digital preservation initiative, which documents and shares Tunisia’s endangered cultural heritage through 3D scanning and immersive storytelling.
