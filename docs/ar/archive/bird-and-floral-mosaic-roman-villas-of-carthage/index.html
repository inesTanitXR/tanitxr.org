Bird and Floral Mosaic – Roman Villas of Carthage

الرئيسية  ›  الأرشيف  ›  Bird and Floral Mosaic – Roman Villas of Carthage
CarthageRoman villas of Carthage🎮 Game-ready available

👐 game-ready optimization by Rachel West

This Roman mosaic, preserved from the ancient villas of Carthage, depicts a finely detailed bird surrounded by flourishing vines, fruits, and foliage. The naturalistic rendering of the bird, paired with the geometric border framing the scene, reflects the Roman taste for combining elegance with everyday symbolism. Birds were a common motif in domestic mosaics, often representing abundance, leisure, and the harmony of nature within the Roman household. The craftsmanship reveals the refined mosaic tradition of Roman Africa, where local artisans blended Mediterranean themes with North African styles, making Carthage a center of cultural and artistic production in antiquity. Location: Roman Villas of Carthage, Tunisia 📌 Part of the Tanit XR Digital Preservation Initiative, dedicated to documenting and preserving Tunisia’s cultural heritage through advanced 3D scanning technologies. 📍 [Carthage, Tunis, Tunisia](.
