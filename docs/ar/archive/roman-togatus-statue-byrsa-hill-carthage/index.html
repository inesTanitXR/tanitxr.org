قرطاجتل بيرصا🎮 جاهز للألعاب available

👐 game-ready optimization by Daniel Gómez

This headless marble statue, discovered on تل بيرصا in قرطاج, depicts a Roman figure draped in a toga. The detailed folds of the garment symbolize civic duty and elite status in Roman society. Although the head and parts of the arms are missing, the craftsmanship of the drapery remains striking. تل بيرصا, once the political and religious heart of قرطاج, was later transformed into a Roman city center after the بوني Wars, leaving behind statues like this one that reflect the blending of بوني وروماني heritage. 📍 [Byrsa, قرطاج, Tunis, Tunisia](.
