Was Queen Elissa real?

She’s the founder of قرطاج in legend: a Phoenician princess from Tyre who escaped a murderous brother, crossed the sea, و built one of the great cities of the ancient world with only the people who accompanied her و hide of an ox. Rome remembered her as Dido. Virgil turned her into a heartbroken queen. Others remembered her as a ruler who chose death over a forced marriage.

But how much of that is history, و how much is story?

While you are here: this session is free, like everything Tanit XR makes. Everyone on the team is a volunteer, so a gift goes straight to preserving Tunisia’s heritage. Donations go through our fiscal sponsor, Florida Community Innovation, a U.S. 501(c)(3), so they are tax-deductible.

The session

One hour, three threads

1

The founding myth و how it changed depending on who was telling it

Elissa of Tyre, Dido of Rome, Virgil’s heartbroken queen, the ruler who chose death over a forced marriage. And the version most أحببتها in Tunisia: the ox hide, cut into thin strips to circle the hill of Byrsa.

2

What archaeology suggests about when و how قرطاج actually began

What the excavations have uncovered, و how it sits beside the stories. Telling what we know apart from what we wish we knew.

3

Why the story of a woman founding a city has lasted nearly 3,000 years

In Tunisia, Elissa و Tanit are still symbols of women’s strength. The hour ends on what Elissa means to us today, with time for your questions along the way.

The speaker

من ستستمعون إليهن

Julia Moreno-Molen

Project manager و volunteer coordinator, Tanit XR

“Hi! I’m Julia Moreno-Molen. I’ll be honest: I’m not an archaeologist. I came to TanitXR as a project manager who cares deeply about preserving و sharing cultural heritage و leaning into learning. As a newbie to the field, I’ve enjoyed reading translations of ancient sources, learning what the excavations have uncovered, و asking a lot of questions about who tells our histories. That’s the spirit of this session. You don’t need credentials to engage seriously with the past. You just need curiosity و a willingness to tell what we know apart from what we wish we knew.”

This session is hosted by Tanit XR, a volunteer-driven organization working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s heritage, together with its fiscal sponsor, Florida Community Innovation (FCI). Tanit XR is built on the idea that anyone with curiosity و care can help keep history alive.

“I can’t wait to see you there!” Julia
