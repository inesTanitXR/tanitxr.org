Walk around it, slowly

That is honestly most of it. Walk a full circle around the object with your phone, then another circle a little higher, then one lower, so every photo overlaps the last. We use Scaniverse, which is free. Nura is showing you the path right now.

The technique is the easy part. Our guide also covers asking permission and taking care of the place you are in.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
