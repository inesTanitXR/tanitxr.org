Send us your scan

Home  ›  Send us your scan

Scanned something, or made something? Send it here and it can join the collection on Explore. Upload the file, paste a link to it, or point us at it on Sketchfab, and tell us what it is so it does not arrive as an object with no story.

Everything is looked at by a person before it appears, so nothing you send goes on the site straight away. Not scanned anything yet? Read the scanning guide first.

What are you sending?
How are you sending it?

Drag your file here

or press to choose one. FBX, GLB, OBJ, PLY, USDZ or a zip, up to 2 GB.

Uploading straight to us is not switched on yet. Paste a link instead and we will fetch it.

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, anything we can download from. Check that we are allowed to open it.

If it is already published, this is all we need.

Scanning with us today?

Three steps, from your phone

  1. Export from Scaniverse. Open your scan, tap Share, then Export Model, then GLB.
  2. Name the file. YourFirstName_ObjectName.glb, for example Maya_CoffeeMug.glb. That is how your name and the object's name reach us.
  3. Send it with the form above, choosing the workshop answer, or email it to ines@tanitxr.org if the file is under 25 MB.

Files up to about 150 MB are fine, we shrink them. Your scan goes on Explore once we have looked at it, under From our workshops.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
