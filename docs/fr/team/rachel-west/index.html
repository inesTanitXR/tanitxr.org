Rachel West

Accueil  ›  Notre équipe  ›  Rachel West
Design 2D et généraliste 3D

Rachel West provides both graphic design and 3D asset support to TanitXR. She graduated from the University of Florida with both her Bachelor's and Master's degree, and now works for the College of Education at the same institution as a Application Developer. In addition, Rachel also serves as an adjunct lecturer, teaching undergraduate courses in 3D modeling and animation. In her work, she specializes in 3D asset creation and designing applications for extended reality platforms. In her free time, Rachel loves to bake and game. She is also a co-head of an on-campus martial arts school, where she regularly volunteers her time to instruct students within the style.

À propos de Tanit XR

Tanit XR est une initiative à but non lucratif qui œuvre à préserver numériquement le patrimoine culturel tunisien avant qu’il ne disparaisse sous l’effet du temps, des intempéries ou de la négligence. Grâce à la numérisation 3D, à des archives numériques ouvertes et à des expériences immersives AR/VR, nous rendons mosaïques, statues et sites historiques accessibles aux élèves, aux chercheurs et au grand public, partout.
