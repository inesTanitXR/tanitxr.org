Bloc architectural sculpté à motifs de laurier

Accueil  ›  Archives  ›  Bloc architectural sculpté à motifs de laurier
ZaghouanTemple des Eaux, Zaghouan🎮 Prêt pour le jeu available

👐 game-ready optimization by Nick Kaufmann

This scan captures a decorated architectural block from the Water Temple of Zaghouan, an ancient Roman sanctuary built in the 2nd century CE under Emperor Hadrian. The temple marked the monumental starting point of the Zaghouan aqueduct, which carried water over 90 kilometers to the city of Carthage. The block is carved with laurel motifs, a recurring symbol in Roman architecture associated with triumph, eternity, and divine favor. Its rhythmic pattern reflects the refined craftsmanship of Roman stonecutters, who combined durability with ornamentation. Though now displaced from its original position, this block once contributed to the temple’s monumental appearance, crowning or lining parts of the sanctuary’s structure. The temple itself was not only a feat of hydraulic engineering but also a sacred site, dedicated to water as both a physical necessity and a divine gift. Location: Temple des Eaux, Zaghouan, Tunisia 📌 Part of the Tanit XR Digital.

À propos de Tanit XR

Tanit XR est une initiative à but non lucratif qui œuvre à préserver numériquement le patrimoine culturel tunisien avant qu’il ne disparaisse sous l’effet du temps, des intempéries ou de la négligence. Grâce à la numérisation 3D, à des archives numériques ouvertes et à des expériences immersives AR/VR, nous rendons mosaïques, statues et sites historiques accessibles aux élèves, aux chercheurs et au grand public, partout.
