Heritage at Risk.
Technology at Hand.

The world’s archaeological sites are vanishing faster than they can be protected. Climate change brings floods, storms and heat that accelerate erosion. Without urgent action, pieces of world history could disappear. With every scan, Tanit XR creates a permanent archive. Even if the physical site is lost, the digital memory survives, for schools, museums and future generations.

More than an archive

One phone, the ruins of Carthage, and now a community

Tanit XR was founded by Ines Said, who grew up in Tunisia surrounded by ruins. Today, we are a global community of volunteers in Tunisia, the U.S., Europe and Africa who meet every week to scan, optimize, teach and learn history, mentor students, and publish and present research. We are building a free, open archive of Tunisia’s heritage, which we hope will be a model for other underrepresented regions.

What we do

Our Approach to Digital Heritage

Scan & Preserve

Volunteers capture statues, mosaics and ruins with their phones. Every scan becomes a permanent, open record.

Explore the Archive →

Optimize & Build

Remote volunteers turn raw scans into game-ready models, AR lessons and our virtual museum.

Visit the virtual museum →

Learn Together

Weekly community calls, history lessons on the sites we scan, and the Splats With Phones workshop.

Join the community →

Mentor & Grow

Interview prep, portfolio reviews and mentoring for students and early-career volunteers, across four continents.

Volunteer with us →

Research & Share

Academic papers, conference presentations, public talks, hackathons and workshops. We publish and share our research and insights with a global audience.

Press & recognition →

Extend Beyond Tunisia

We are building a global framework for collaborative digital heritage. It started in Tunisia, inspired by Tanit, the goddess of protection. We are now extending the model globally, partnering with the Unique Mappers in Nigeria and others.

About Tanit XR

Tanit XR is a nonprofit initiative working to digitally preserve Tunisia’s cultural heritage before it disappears to time, weather, or neglect. Through 3D scanning, an open digital archive, and immersive AR/VR experiences, we make mosaics, statues, and historic sites accessible to students, researchers, and the public everywhere.
